An independent evaluation

OCEAN adds tendencies—not destiny.

The Big Five can provide a disciplined vocabulary for personality variation. It does not turn a generated participant into customer evidence.

The verdict

Use OCEAN as an optional variance scaffold. Do not use it as a behavior oracle.

Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, and Neuroticism describe broad, continuous tendencies. They can help a simulation avoid one generic voice, but they do not independently determine goals, expertise, decision rights, workflow constraints, or what a customer will do.

Why it is useful

Continuous variation

The dimensions are sliders rather than fixed types, allowing controlled comparisons without invented biographies.

Research foundation

The Big Five has extensive psychometric research, including facet-level instruments and substantial cross-cultural study.

Testable influence

Keep the task constant, change only one trait, and inspect whether style changes without new facts or authority appearing.

How Synthetic Users describes it

Synthetic Users publicly describes OCEAN as one layer in a larger architecture that also includes behavioral signals, population calibration, situational context, retrieval from customer-provided data, and multiple models. Its explanation says traits influence tendencies rather than deterministically fixing choices.

Its more recent material describes neural-validation work as early and separately cautions that representational alignment would not automatically validate downstream behavioral predictions. Claims about its proprietary data, calibration, parity, and implementation have not been independently tested by this project.

The LLM-specific caution

Published experiments show that language models can produce self-reports and text aligned with assigned Big Five characteristics. Other results show unusually clean personality structures and reduced robustness as roles become more complex.

A convincing personality performance can therefore be stronger than its real-world behavioral validity. An assumed OCEAN score is still an assumption.

If a consequential product decision changes only because an assumed personality score changed, take that decision to human research.
