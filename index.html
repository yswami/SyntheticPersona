Know what each layer contributes

Five sliders, not five boxes

OCEAN adds tendencies—not destiny

The Big Five describes broad, continuous personality dimensions. It can help diversify how a reviewer communicates and explores a situation, but behavior still depends on role, incentives, task, context, and evidence.
